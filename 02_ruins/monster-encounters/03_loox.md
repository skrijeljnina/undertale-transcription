## Beat

Gives 7 XP and 5 gold if killed, and 0 XP and 5 gold if spared.

If killed and picked on once (1), it gives 12 XP, 5 gold, and your LOVE increases. If killed and picked on twice (2), it gives 17 XP, 5 gold, and your LOVE increases. If killed and picked on thrice (3), it gives 22 XP, 5 gold, and your LOVE increases.
