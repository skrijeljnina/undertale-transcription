## Beat

Gives 3 XP and 2 gold if killed, and 0 XP and 2 gold if spared.

### 1st Froggit

If Toriel scares away the 1st Froggit you meet, you get 0 XP and 0 gold. If you kill it, it gives 10 XP, 20 gold, and your LOVE increases.
